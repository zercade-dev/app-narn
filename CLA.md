## Adaptations

The clause text above is the Apache ICLA v2.2 with the party names changed. For the
avoidance of doubt, these are all the changes:

1. **Party names.** "The Apache Software Foundation" becomes Zercade, and every
   occurrence of "the Foundation" becomes "the Licensor" — the same party named in
   [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
2. **Submission instructions.** The Apache address for returning a signed copy is
   replaced by the Licensor's.
3. **The reciprocal sentence in the preamble is removed.** Apache's original has the
   Foundation undertake not to use Contributions "in a way that is contrary to the
   public benefit or inconsistent with its nonprofit status and bylaws". The Licensor
   is not a nonprofit and has no bylaws, so that undertaking cannot be made here and
   is not made. Nothing is put in its place: the sentence that follows it in the
   original — You reserve all right, title, and interest in Your Contributions — is
   kept unchanged.
4. **The form fields.** Apache's fields exist to populate an Apache account and a
   public foundation profile, so the Apache-id, notify-project and public-name fields
   are dropped. Name, postal address, country and e-mail are kept; an optional GitHub
   username is added.
5. **The closing note is newly written, not edited.** Apache's form ends "This is a
   legal contract containing Personally Identifiable Information. Please refer to
   https://s.apache.org/cla-privacy-policy for the policy governing how this
   information is used and shared." Apache's policy does not govern anything here, so
   that sentence is dropped and the one above it is written fresh, recording only that
   the Licensor keeps the signed copy. Stated plainly, because it is the one place
   this adaptation gives you less than the original: **this project publishes no
   privacy notice** covering the details the form asks for.
6. **The version designator.** Apache's form is V2.2; this adaptation carries its own
   version, v1.0, so that a later revision of either can be identified.

Clauses 1 to 8 are otherwise word for word.

**On the Corporate CLA in clause 4.** That clause offers a third path for a
contributor whose employer holds rights in their work: the employer executes a
separate Corporate CLA. No such document exists here yet. If your employer needs one,
say so when you email this agreement to the address above and one will be drawn up —
the clause is left untouched rather than deleted, so the option stays open.
