# Category Tab

## Overview

**Categories** group related strings (for example UI labels, dialogue, item names). They help you organize the project, filter the string table, and — importantly — drive **routing rules** and **batch grouping**, so categorized content can be sent to the right module and translated together in context.

## Browsing categories

The **Category** tab lists your current categories with an entry count each. Select one to see its entries and to edit its **description** — a short note describing what the category groups.

## Assigning categories

* Assign categories to individual entries from the **Translations** tab (right-click a row → *Edit Categories*), or in bulk by selecting rows there.
* Or generate them automatically (below).

## Generate with AI

Click **Generate with AI** to have a model read your source strings, propose categories, and assign entries to them.

1. Pick the **module** and **model**.
2. Optionally let it also assign entries into your **existing** categories.
3. Click **Generate** — it runs in the background, so you can leave the tab and come back.
4. **Review suggestions**: uncheck any you don't want, then **Apply selected**.

Nothing changes until you apply, so AI suggestions are always yours to confirm first.
