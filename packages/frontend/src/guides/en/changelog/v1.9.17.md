## v1.9.17 — 2026-07-04

You can now set a tone on individual entries in the Compare tab to steer AI translations and reviews.

* You can now set a tone (like "Playful" or "Formal") on individual entries in the Compare tab, with autocomplete from tones already used in your project. Tone steers AI translations and reviews, and can be used to group batches, route entries to specific models, or filter the Multi-language Text tab — just like categories.
