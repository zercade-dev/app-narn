## v1.23.0 — 2026-07-13

The Changelog page is now easier to skim: recent releases show in full, and everything older folds away until you want it.

- Each release now leads with a one-line highlight of its most important change, so you can scan the history at a glance.
- Older releases are tucked behind a "Show older releases" button; open it to see condensed one-line entries, and click any one to expand its full details.
- The newest handful of releases still appear in full by default.
