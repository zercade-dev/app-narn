## v1.9.3 — 2026-07-02

Relink AI retranslate now gives the model the full picture across every language, and rate limits are handled properly across all AI runs.

* Relink AI retranslate now gives the model the full picture: every language's translation from the orphaned entry plus the target entry's own translations (captured before the relink), so batch runs no longer lose context for most languages.
* Rate limits are handled properly across all AI runs: quota errors from Google AI are now recognized as rate limits, and background runs wait out the provider's cool-down and retry instead of failing every language immediately.
