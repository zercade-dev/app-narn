## v1.22.0 — 2026-07-12

On hosted deployments, projects now carry a clear @alias/name label everywhere, in the project switcher and once a project is open.

- Projects now carry a clear **`@alias/name` label** everywhere — your own projects show your alias (or `@me` until you pick one), and shared projects always show the owner's alias, both in the project switcher and once a project is open.
- Your projects and projects shared with you now have **distinct colours** in the switcher, so it's easy to tell at a glance which is which.
- **Joining a project** moved into the **New project** panel: it's now a simple "Create new / Join" tab switch instead of a separate menu item.
- A handful of short, self-referential words (like "me" in various languages) are **reserved** and can't be taken as an alias, keeping the `@me` label unambiguous.
