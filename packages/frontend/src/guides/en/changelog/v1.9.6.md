## v1.9.6 — 2026-07-03

Category generation now works with self-hosted LLM endpoints on your local network.

* Category generation now works with self-hosted LLM endpoints reached over plain `http` on your local network — the same endpoints translation and review already accept.
* Approving a reviewed translation into translation memory now keeps its variables and tags in the right order; a rare case with reordered placeholders could previously store them mismatched.
* The back-translation preview no longer momentarily appears under the wrong entry when you switch entries while it is still loading.
