## v1.9.13 — 2026-07-04

Source AI review now remembers your decisions, resuming at the first entry still waiting for you.

* Source AI review now remembers your decisions: approving an entry sticks across reloads, and review resumes at the first entry still waiting for you. The old "flag" action is replaced by "ignore", which removes an entry from the review list for good.
* New review scope: run a source review over only the entries you've never reviewed before (the new default), or everything — with counts shown for both.
* A small reminder in the review card makes clear that approving never edits your source text — apply corrections in your own content.
* The "vault locked" chip in the console is now clickable and takes you straight to the unlock dialog.
* Long entries in the Orphans relink view no longer push the buttons out of sight or cause sideways scrolling.
