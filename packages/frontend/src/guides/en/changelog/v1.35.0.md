## v1.35.0 — 2026-07-17

The Translations table got a big usability upgrade: it now fills your whole screen (no more huge empty area below the rows), each row is half as tall with its status, category, and visibility shown on one line, and the filter bar arranges itself neatly on smaller screens. Screens without a selected project now offer a button to pick one, and several accessibility improvements landed across the app, including clearer contrast for the vault-locked indicator.
