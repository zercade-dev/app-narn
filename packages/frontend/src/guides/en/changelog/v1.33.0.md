## v1.33.0 — 2026-07-17

A new Settings page, with four UI themes to pick from.

- Four UI themes — Default, Pixel, Techno, and Minimal — each with light and dark variants.
- Pick your look from the new Settings entry in the sidebar; your choice is remembered per device.
- The language and dark-mode controls moved into Settings, and Sign out now lives on the Account page.
