## v1.55.0 — 2026-07-27

The Stage details and Text Styler AI assistants now let you dial in how much the model "thinks" before it answers — a new control sits right next to the model picker in each assistant's settings. Both also show a clear "thinking" indicator with a live elapsed-time counter while waiting on that first response, so a slower, more thoughtful model no longer looks like it's just hung. And when the Stage details assistant proposes an edit, the preview card now includes a short note on why the change is an improvement, not just the change itself.
