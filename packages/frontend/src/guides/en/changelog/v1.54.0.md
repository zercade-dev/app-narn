## v1.54.0 — 2026-07-26

Achievement pairing now opens a proper picker. Instead of a single guessed suggestion, you get a searchable list of every counterpart you could link to, showing at a glance which ones are already paired. Linking works both ways, and if the counterpart has no Name/Description tag yet, it gets the right one automatically.

The Stage details assistant reads properly now — replies are formatted instead of showing raw markup, and suggested text no longer appears twice. It sticks more closely to what you asked for, and when a reply comes back with nothing you can use, there's a one-click retry. You can also pick which AI model it chats with, independently of the one used for translations.

Row action buttons in the Compare tab are more compact and stay put instead of drifting out of line.
