Routing, Glossary, Quality, Compare and AI review now offer a short tour the first time you open them in Advanced mode.

- Each tour takes two or three steps and explains what the area is for and why to use it.
- The offer is a small card you can dismiss; it never comes back once you say no thanks, and you can turn tour offers off in Settings.
- All tours, and the guided first run, can be started again from the Guide.
