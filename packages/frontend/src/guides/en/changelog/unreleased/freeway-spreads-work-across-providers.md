NARN Freeway now leans on your roomiest free allowances first, so the small ones last longer instead of being spent on routine work.

- Providers with a small daily allowance are worked down last, so they are still there once the larger ones run low.
- Runs are less likely to end up "Waiting for free quota" while another provider still has plenty of its allowance left.
- Many free providers with a generous allowance are also faster, so runs often finish sooner as well.
