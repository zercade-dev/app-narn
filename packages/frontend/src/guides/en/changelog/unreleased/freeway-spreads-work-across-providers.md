NARN Freeway now leans on your roomiest free allowances first, saving the small ones for when they are the only provider that can do the job.

- Providers with a small daily allowance are kept back for the work that needs them, rather than being spent on routine strings.
- Runs are less likely to end up "Waiting for free quota" while another provider still has plenty of its allowance left.
- Many free providers with a generous allowance are also faster, so runs often finish sooner as well.
