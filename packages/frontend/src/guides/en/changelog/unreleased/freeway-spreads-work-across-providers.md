NARN Freeway now spreads translation work across your free providers instead of using up the tightest daily allowances first.

- Providers with a small daily allowance are saved for the work that needs them, rather than being spent on routine strings.
- Runs are less likely to stop early with "no free capacity left today" while other providers still have plenty.
- Many free providers with a generous allowance are also faster, so runs often finish sooner as well.
