Failed runs now explain themselves, a new bell keeps track of what happened, and the console log reads like plain sentences.

- When a translation or review run fails, the log and the notification now say why in plain words, such as a rejected key, a rate or allowance limit, or a timeout, and offer the fix where there is one.
- A bell on the console bar opens a sidebar of notifications for runs that started, finished, finished with problems or failed, each with a "View in Activity" button that jumps to the run.
- Failures stay unread until you act on them or dismiss them, and account messages appear in the same list.
- The console log now reads as plain sentences by default, with a Friendly/Raw switch when you want the raw log.
- The console bar shows one line of what is happening right now, for example "Translating into French: 38 of 250".
- Cloud only: project owners get an hourly summary of what their collaborators changed, sent only when someone actually worked.
