NARN Freeway now reviews translations the same careful way it translates them, and spends much less of your daily free allowance doing it.

- Reviews now avoid free models measured weak in the languages being reviewed, so a language is checked by a model that struggles with it only when there is no alternative.
- Review batches are now sized to the model actually serving them instead of a fixed size, preventing oversized requests on long text and stopping small reviews from burning through a whole day's free allowance.
- Review scores now feed back into how free models are ranked, so NARN Freeway learns which model translates each language well, and it leaves out a model's score for its own translations wherever it can tell.
