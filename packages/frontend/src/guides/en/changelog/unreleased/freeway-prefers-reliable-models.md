NARN Freeway now prefers models that are actually passing quality checks, instead of whichever free model has the most quota left.

- A model whose recent translations keep failing the quality gate is pushed behind healthier models, even when it has a much larger free allowance.
- Models of equal quality are still balanced by remaining quota, so work keeps spreading across providers as before.
- Nothing is taken out of rotation: a struggling model is still used when it is the best available option for a language.
