The guide can now test-translate a file that is already fully translated, and put your translations back afterwards.

- When your file already has every translation, the guide offers to retranslate 3 entries so you can see a translation happen.
- After that test, the guide walks you to the restore point saved just before it, or you can keep the new translations.
- Simple mode now has a short Backups page for making, restoring and downloading backups.
- Providers with a free tier are listed first when you choose a provider, and the guide talks about free tiers rather than free keys.
- The import review is shorter: one line per warning, with the language list for raw line breaks one click away.
