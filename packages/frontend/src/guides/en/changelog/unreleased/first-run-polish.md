The first-run guide now shows how much is left, starts with a quick three-string test, and the console speaks plainly.

- Each step of the guided setup shows a small progress bar, so you can see how far into it you are.
- The guide first translates a quick test of three strings, then offers to translate the rest now or later.
- While a translation runs, a progress card shows how much is done and roughly how much time is left.
- The console now shows one line per language when it finishes, instead of a line for every string.
- Simple mode is tidier: advanced filters, bulk actions and run options stay out of the way until you switch to Advanced.
- The Guide lists every tour with your progress, and you can continue a tour where you left off.
- New Glossaries and Orphans tours; the Orphans tour can show you a practice orphan and put it back.
- Flipping to Advanced at the end of the guided setup starts a short tour of what each new section is for.
