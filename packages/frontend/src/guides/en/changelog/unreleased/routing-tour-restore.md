Leaving the Advanced routing rules tour halfway no longer leaves languages without a provider.

- The tour has you edit real rules. If you leave it while a language that had a provider would have none, or with a rule that has no provider, your earlier routing comes back, and a short notice says so.
- If your rules already work, for example because a provider for the other languages is already there, they are kept, whichever step you left on.
- Switching to Simple during the tour starts from your routing as it was before the tour, unless your new rules already work.
