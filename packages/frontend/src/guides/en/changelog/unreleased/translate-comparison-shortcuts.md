The Comparison view's keyboard-shortcut help, its per-cell tooltips and its status chips now appear in your language.

- The shortcuts popover was still English-only; it is now translated along with the rest of the interface.
- The re-translate, mark-reviewed, clear and edit buttons on each translation cell now have translated tooltips and screen-reader labels too, worded to match the shortcuts popover exactly.
- The "translated", "reviewed" and "needs review" chips in each cell are translated as well, using the same wording as the matching filter.
- The key names themselves (T, R, C, Enter, Esc and the arrows) stay as they are — those are the keys on your keyboard, not words.
