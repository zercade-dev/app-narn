Relinking a string no longer sends its Pseudo Test text to a paid AI provider.

- The AI retranslate pass that follows a relink now skips the Pseudo Test language, the same way every other translation route does.
- Your pseudo-localized text stays exactly as it was, instead of being replaced by a model's answer and billed for.
- The run's progress count and the languages it lists now cover only the languages it actually retranslates.
