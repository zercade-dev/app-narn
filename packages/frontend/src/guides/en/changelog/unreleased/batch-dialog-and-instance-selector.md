Run dialogs are simpler by default, and NARN Freeway can now be pointed at a specific provider setup instead of always picking automatically.

- Translation, review, and generation dialogs now show just the choice that matters, with the tuning options tucked behind an "Advanced options" tick that NARN remembers between runs.
- Anything you set while Advanced options is open keeps applying even after you collapse it back down.
- NARN Freeway can now be pointed at a specific one of a provider's setups, chosen by name, instead of always picking automatically; leaving it on Automatic keeps today's behavior.
