NARN Freeway now plans a run's full length ahead of time, so its best free providers no longer look falsely out of capacity.

- Runs used to see each provider's per-minute allowance as if the whole run happened in one instant, making high-quality providers look drained and pushing work onto weaker fallbacks.
- The planner now spends that allowance across the run's real timeline, so scarce top-tier providers get used the way they're actually available.
- Daily and pooled limits are unaffected — only how the per-minute allowance is planned.
