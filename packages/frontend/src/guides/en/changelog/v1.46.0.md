## v1.46.0 — 2026-07-17

Theme polish across the board, driven by your feedback. Settings now leads with the interface language and shows a live preview strip — sample buttons, status labels, and a text field — so you can see exactly what each theme looks like before picking it. The Techno theme's light mode got a real identity of its own: a bright, cool workspace paired with a dark neon sidebar, instead of looking like the default theme. Theme fonts now reach much further — buttons, tabs, and every heading level pick up the theme's typeface, including the manual review cards and "review in progress" panels. Status labels everywhere (like "translated" and "needs review" in the Compare tab) now follow the active theme's colors instead of fixed ones. In the project list on hosted deployments, your own projects and shared projects are now tinted across the whole row rather than a small label. And module cards in Global Config no longer push their buttons out of view when an instance has a long name.
