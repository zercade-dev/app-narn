## v1.59.0 — 2026-07-30

The Text Styler's AI assistant now reads the way it should.

- Replies are formatted properly instead of showing the raw code-block marks around a suggestion.
- Each suggestion arrives as its own card with a one-line explanation of what the assistant changed and why — the same reasoning the stage details assistant already gives you.
- Every suggestion now has a Discard button next to Apply.
- Once you pick one, the suggestion folds away into a single quiet line, so a long conversation stays readable instead of turning into a wall of options you already dealt with.
