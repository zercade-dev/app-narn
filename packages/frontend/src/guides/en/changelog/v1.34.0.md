## v1.34.0 — 2026-07-17

The Text Styler moves into your project, and assistant chats now show their token usage.

- The Text Styler is now a tab inside each project, with its draft text saved per project — your existing draft is carried over to the first project you open it in. Your color palette stays shared across projects.
- Assistant chats (Text Styler and stage details) now appear in the project's Activity tab as a single entry per conversation, showing turns, tokens, and estimated cost — and the entry grows as the chat continues.
- Chat sessions are never listed as cancellable runs; they simply record what each conversation cost you.
