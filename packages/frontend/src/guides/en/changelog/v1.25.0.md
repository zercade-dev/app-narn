## v1.25.0 — 2026-07-13

The Quality tab has been redesigned with richer, clearer charts.

- A new overview shows your overall pass rate as a donut alongside the headline number and pass/fail totals.
- Pass rate by language is now a sorted bar chart (worst first), and a new chart ranks the most common issue types across the project.
- Issues per language, source, and AI module are shown as a colour-intensity heatmap instead of small dots, and clicking a language or source still jumps straight to the affected strings.
