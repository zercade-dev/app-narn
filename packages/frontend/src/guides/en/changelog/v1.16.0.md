## v1.16.0 — 2026-07-10

New: review suggestions you can steer — discard or re-request an AI suggestion with your own guidance.

* **New: review suggestions you can steer.** Discard an AI review suggestion you don't like, or re-request one with your own guidance (e.g. "keep the brand name in English").
* **New: dialogs remember your choices.** Translation, AI review, source review, glossary and category dialogs now reopen with the settings you last used on this browser.
* Clear an entry's "needs review" or "reviewed" status straight from the right-click menu in the Compare tab.
* The "Needs review" filter now catches every translation that hasn't been reviewed yet — not only the ones flagged automatically.
