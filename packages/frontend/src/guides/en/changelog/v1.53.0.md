## v1.53.0 — 2026-07-22

Another round of fixes from your feedback.

- The Text Styler's AI assistant no longer comes up empty when the credential vault is locked: opening it while locked left it looking dead with nothing in the console.
- It now re-checks your providers the moment you unlock, tells you exactly why it can't chat yet — vault locked, missing credentials, or nothing configured — with a one-click way to unlock, and never fails silently again.
- The Compare tab's action icons are now clean, crisp icons in every theme instead of the chunky pixel versions.
- Badges and the review-queue language pill are readable again in the Pixel theme, and the Settings preview shows it.
- The glossary's edit, delete, and add buttons stay pinned in view instead of hiding behind a horizontal scroll.
- Long provider names on the Global Config cards no longer get clipped mid-word.
- The Copilot setup guide is hidden on hosted deployments, where Copilot isn't available.
- A thorough pass over the help guides corrected stale instructions — the Docker guide now reflects the Postgres setup, the Config guide points at the new Data tab for languages and CSV, and a handful of renamed buttons and tabs are now accurate.
