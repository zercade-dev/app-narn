## v1.48.0 — 2026-07-17

Settings and configuration now save themselves. The Save buttons are gone from the settings, quality-check, and routing panels — every change is kept automatically as you make it, with a small "Saved" note so you know it landed.

Routing rules gained a "Default tone" setting — give a rule a tone once and every line it handles that doesn't already have its own tone will use it, while lines with their own tone keep it.

On hosted deployments the account area is simpler, with the separate billing tab removed. Assistant chat and general reliability there got steadier too: the app now recovers gracefully when a signed-in session quietly expires, instead of appearing to hang.
